Submit one or more recipe ideas for validation and ranking. Each is checked against hard
constraints (allergies, exclusions, pantry feasibility, required equipment, cook time) — a
candidate that fails is rejected and explained, not silently dropped. Candidates that pass are
scored and returned ranked, favoring recipes that use ingredients close to expiring and that
you can make with what's already in the pantry. Submit all your ideas in one call rather than
one at a time. State how many portions each recipe's ingredient list is written for — this is
used later to scale ingredient quantities when the user reports how many portions they actually
cooked.
